IELTS Speaking Part 2 mavzulari (50 ta kartochka)

Har bir kartochka uchun 1 daqiqa tayyorgarlik va 1–2 daqiqa nutq. Ostida shu mavzu bo‘yicha Part 3 savollari. Bular Sellzen’ning o‘z mashq kartochkalari (rasmiy IELTS savollari emas).

Tasodifiy kartochka + taymer

1. Describe a person who has helped you.

  • who this person is
  • how you know them
  • how they helped you
  • and explain how you felt about it.
Part 3 savollari
  1. Why do some people find it hard to ask for help?
  2. Should schools teach students to help others?
  3. How has technology changed the way people help each other?

2. Describe a place you would like to visit in the future.

  • where it is
  • how you would get there
  • what you would do there
  • and explain why you want to go.
Part 3 savollari
  1. Why do people like to visit famous places?
  2. What are the positive and negative effects of tourism?
  3. Will people travel more or less in the future?

3. Describe a skill you learned that was useful.

Part 3 savollari

  2. Is it better to learn skills at school or at work?
  3. Will robots replace human skills in the future?
