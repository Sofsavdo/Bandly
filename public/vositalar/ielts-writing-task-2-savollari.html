IELTS Writing Task 2 savollari (39 ta)

Insho turi bo‘yicha guruhlangan Task 2 savollari: kamida 250 so‘z, 40 daqiqa. Bular Sellzen’ning o‘z mashq savollari (rasmiy IELTS savollari emas). Inshoingizni Sellzen’da yozib, 4 mezon bo‘yicha AI bahosini olishingiz mumkin.

Agree / disagree (fikringiz) — 10 ta

  1. Some people think that modern technology has made communication between people worse. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  2. Some people say that individuals can do little to protect the environment and that only governments can solve the problem. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  3. Some people believe that schools should concentrate on teaching practical skills rather than academic subjects. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  4. Some people think that private cars should be banned from city centres. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  5. Advertising aimed at children should be banned. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  6. Governments spend too much money on space exploration when there are serious problems on Earth. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  7. Children should start learning a foreign language at primary school rather than secondary school. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  8. Robots and artificial intelligence will soon replace most human jobs. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  9. Studying the past is a waste of time; students should focus on subjects that prepare them for the future. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  10. Public transport should be free in all cities. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Positive or negative development (baholash) — 6 ta

  1. More and more people now work from home. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of this trend and give your own opinion.
  2. Several governments have introduced a tax on sugary drinks in order to improve public health. Do the advantages of this policy outweigh the disadvantages?
  3. Online courses are becoming more popular than traditional classes. Do the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages?
  4. Some students take a year off between school and university to work or travel. Do the advantages of this outweigh the disadvantages?
  5. International tourism has grown rapidly in recent decades. Do the benefits for local communities outweigh the problems?
  6. In some countries people hardly use cash any more. Is this a positive or negative development?

Discuss both views (ikki fikr) — 9 ta

  1. Some people believe that university education should be free for everyone, while others think students should pay. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  2. Some companies are introducing a four-day working week with no reduction in pay, believing that it makes employees more productive. Others think it would harm business. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  3. Some people think that traditional customs should be preserved at all costs, while others believe that it is natural for them to disappear as society changes. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  4. Some people believe that the main purpose of prison is to punish criminals, while others think it should be to rehabilitate them. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  5. Some people think museums should be free for everyone, while others believe visitors should pay. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  6. Some people believe that schoolchildren should not be given homework, while others think it is essential. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  7. Some people think the best way to reduce crime is to give longer prison sentences, while others believe education is more effective. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  8. Some people say governments should spend money on professional sport, while others think it should be spent on public sports facilities. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  9. Some people believe children should be taught to compete, while others think they should learn to cooperate. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Causes and effects (sabab va oqibat) — 7 ta

  1. In many countries, more people are moving to big cities. What are the causes of this, and what problems can it create?
  2. Many countries still depend heavily on fossil fuels for their energy. What are the reasons for this, and what could be done to encourage the use of renewable energy?
  3. In many countries, young people are leaving the countryside to live in cities, and farming communities are shrinking. What are the causes of this trend, and what effects does it have?
  4. People are eating more fast food than ever before. Why is this happening, and what effects does it have on individuals and society?
  5. Young people spend several hours a day on social media. What are the reasons for this, and what effects does it have on them?
  6. Many young people today want to become famous rather than have a traditional career. Why is this, and is it a positive or negative development?
  7. In many developing countries young people are leaving rural areas to work in cities. What are the causes of this, and what effects does it have on the countryside?

Problem / solution (muammo va yechim) — 7 ta

  1. In many countries, children now spend more time using screens than playing outdoors. What problems does this cause, and what can be done to address them?
  2. Many skilled workers leave poorer countries to find better jobs abroad. What problems does this cause for their home countries, and what can be done about it?
  3. In many countries the proportion of older people is increasing. What problems does this cause, and what can be done to solve them?
  4. Traffic congestion is a growing problem in many cities. What are the main causes, and what measures could be taken to reduce it?
  5. Plastic waste is damaging oceans and wildlife around the world. What are the causes of this problem, and what solutions can you suggest?
  6. Childhood obesity is increasing in many parts of the world. What are the main causes, and what can parents and schools do about it?
  7. Families spend less time together than they used to. Why is this happening, and how can families be encouraged to spend more time together?

Ingliz tili darajangizni bepul aniqlang
